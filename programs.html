Two paths. One Legacy Year.

One purpose.

The money opens the door; the relational investment is the heart of the program.

Businesses may change. Ideas may evolve. But an investment in a person can multiply for a lifetime.

The Entrepreneur Fellowship is not a bet on whether someone's first business becomes wildly successful.

Likewise, the Education Scholarship is not simply an investment in a degree. The Foundation invests in character, confidence, purpose, leadership, relationships, connection, growth, and the potential for that person to someday invest in someone else.

Legacy Education Scholarship

Application and reference deadline: April 2, 2027, at 11:59 PM.

Working Eligibility

  • Graduating high-school senior through junior in college
  • Resides within the Foundation’s Region. Defined as a one-hour radius of St. Louis / St. Charles County. Those outside the region remain eligible, provided they can attend required in-person leadership and mentorship meetings every other month.
  • Pursuing an approved postsecondary education or career-training pathway.
  • Does not need to identify as Christian, but understands the Foundation is openly Christian and the Legacy Year is rooted in Christian servant-leadership principles.

Legacy Entrepreneur Fellowship

Application and reference deadline: April 2, 2027, at 11:59 PM.

Working Eligibility

  • Under age 35 at the application deadline.
  • Resides within the Foundation’s Region. Defined as a one-hour radius of St. Louis / St. Charles County. Those outside the region remain eligible, provided they can attend required in-person leadership and mentorship meetings every other month.
  • Has a defined business concept and written business plan.
  • Has begun meaningful early-stage work toward launch.
  • Can clearly identify the customer or need, revenue model, startup budget, current progress, next steps, and how Foundation support would be used.
  • Does not need to identify as Christian, but understands the Foundation is openly Christian and the Legacy Year is rooted in Christian servant-leadership principles.

Who We Are Looking For

We aren't only asking, “What have you accomplished?” We are asking, “What could happen if someone invested in you?”

We are not simply looking for the most accomplished applicant. We are looking for character, integrity, servant leadership, purpose, perseverance, potential, willingness to learn, and someone who has shown that they will show up for others.

Volunteer Mentors

The Foundation selects each mentor intentionally after getting to know the recipient. There is no generic six-person curriculum.

A mentor's role is relational:

  • Listen to the recipient's story and why.
  • Share honestly from personal experience, including hard lessons.
  • Encourage what they see in the recipient.
  • Challenge them to grow or think differently, an accountability partner for 2 months.
  • Connect them to a person, resource, opportunity, or idea when appropriate.
  • Help them think about purpose, leadership, service, and how their influence can benefit others.

What We Hope Changes

By the end of the Legacy Year, the Foundation hopes to see:

  • Greater confidence.
  • Personal and leadership growth.
  • Greater clarity about direction and purpose.
  • A stronger ability to articulate where they are going and why.
  • Meaningful new relationships and a broader network.
  • A deeper understanding of servant leadership.
  • Experience showing up and serving alongside others.
  • Greater willingness to ask for help and make purposeful connections.
  • A desire to multiply what was invested in them.

The Multiplication Model

  1. SeeKnow the person. Hear their story.
  2. InvestFinancial support + belief.
  3. ConnectPut meaningful people around them.
  4. GrowConfidence • Character • Purpose • Leadership
  5. ServeShow up for someone else.
  6. ReturnCome back. Tell the story. Stay connected.
  7. MultiplyInvest in someone coming behind you.
