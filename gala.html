The Shaun Duvall Legacy Foundation was created to glorify God by investing in and developing the next generation of servant leaders and entrepreneurs through education, mentorship, relationships, service, and opportunity. Shaun believed in showing up for people, encouraging entrepreneurs, building meaningful relationships, leading by example, and pointing the credit back to God.

Our goal is not simply to preserve that legacy – it is to multiply it.

Examples of contributions we may consider

  • Artwork

  • Experiences
  • Professional services needed for the Gala

Why goods or services partnerships are structured separately

Donated goods or services help create meaningful fundraising value while allowing the Foundation to preserve cash-sponsorship and ticket revenue. Recognition and benefits are based on the accepted contributed value and are separate from cash sponsorship levels.
